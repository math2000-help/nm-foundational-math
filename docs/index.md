# Foundational Math Intensive

**Nuclear Medicine Program | Workshop Guide with Solutions**

!!! info "How to use this guide"

    This guide is the main handout for the workshop. We will work through the examples together, step by step. Each topic has a key idea, worked examples with every step shown, practice (“Try”) problems for you to do on your own, calculator tips where they matter, and the common mistakes to watch for. Under each Try problem, click **Show answer** to check your work.

    Your setup line is what gets graded, not only the final answer. For every problem, write your setup as a fraction, show your units canceling, and label your answer with its unit. An answer with no unit earns no credit.

    On quizzes and exams you will get a formula sheet with formulas only. No constants are provided, so you must be able to recall the constants listed on the Constants page. For any other question about grading or the formula sheet, ask your instructor.

    We may not get to every problem in the workshop. The Math Center can help with follow-up practice.
